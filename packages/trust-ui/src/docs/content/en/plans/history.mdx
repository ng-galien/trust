Every accepted execution leaves a **snapshot**: the Check, its Facts, the verdict, the reason, and the checklist delta it caused (newly satisfied, reopened, unchanged). Snapshots are immutable and deduplicated; the history is the audit trail of what was established, when, from what.

## Where else the history shows

- **Overview** — the latest verdicts.
- **Plan page → History** — the latest revision change (added, removed, satisfied, reopened) and, in expert mode, the revisions.
- **Check details** (Plan page) — the verdicts of one Check, newest first; in expert mode its attempts.

## Revisions

A Plan revision is created by an accepted execution (a verdict) or by an agent declaration. A revision may add Checks (a declared collection expands `on each` Checks), remove Checks (their context disappeared), satisfy one, reopen dependents. Revisions are numbered from 1 and never rewritten.

<Callout kind="rule">
  Deleting a dry-run erases its revisions and verdicts; a live Plan is never deleted. Facts themselves are content-addressed and kept.
</Callout>

<Details title="What a row is" expert>
`history.list { filter?: { plan, procedure, mode, verdict, since, until }, cursor?, limit? }` → snapshots newest first: `snapshotId`, `calculatedAt`, `plan`, `mode`, `procedure`, `checkUri`, `checkName`, `target`, `operation`, `attemptHandle`, `verdict`, `reasonCode`, `reason`, `factCount`, `checklistDelta`. Cursors are signed and bound to their filter. In expert mode the row shows `reasonCode · attempt <handle>`.
</Details>
