The **Procedures** entry of the sidebar opens the catalog. Selecting a Procedure opens its page over the catalog; the tab and the selection (a Scenario, a Check or a link of the graph) are in the URL.

### Overview

The Procedure in words: description, the Plan inputs an engagement must give, then each Scenario in dependency order with its Checks. In expert mode a block opens with the full contract of every Check: Input bindings, expectation table, materialized values.

### Source

<Screenshot id="procedure-source" caption="Source tab." legend={{
  "editor": "The Gherkin source, compiled on every change against the Operation catalog. Completions propose the sentences and the Operations of the catalog.",
  "editor.format": "Format source: re-flows long Check sentences onto continuation lines.",
  "overlay.status": "Compiles / does not compile, with the message and the line.",
}} />

- The **Procedure feature** completion inserts a skeleton; **Context role**, **Scenario**, **Check** and **Use `<operation>`** insert the sentences, with the Operations of the catalog.
- **Publish** compiles once more on the runtime and stores a new revision `procedure@version`. Publish is available only when the source compiles and differs from the published one; a version already published is refused — raise `@version`.
- A published version never changes: the interface has no delete or overwrite for a Procedure.

The graph is the dependency structure the runtime uses: **needs** is what must be validated first; **resets** is the cascade — the Checks reopened when the selection gets a new verdict. On a Plan page the same graph shows the live state of each Check.

### Simulate

<Screenshot id="procedure-walk" caption="Simulate tab: the Scenario order, without any Plan." legend={{
  "walk.controls": "Advance to the next actionable Scenario, or reset. Progress in Scenarios.",
  "walk.scenarios": "Every Scenario with its Checks and success reasons; complete, actionable, or waiting for its prerequisites.",
}} />

Simulate executes nothing and creates nothing: it plays the Scenario order to read a Procedure. Rehearsing with Facts is a **dry-run** ([Plans, Sessions and dry-runs](/docs/plans)).

### Contract JSON (expert)

The compiled revision: roles, Scenarios, Checks with their bindings and predicates, digests, and the embedded Operations. It is what a Plan is engaged from.

## Creating, duplicating, publishing

<Steps>
  <Step title="New procedure">From the catalog header or the sidebar. The Source tab opens on a template; the status line shows the first diagnostic until the source compiles.</Step>
  <Step title="Duplicate">A new source pre-filled with the published one; change `@procedure` or raise `@version` before publishing.</Step>
  <Step title="Publish">Enabled once the source compiles. The runtime compiles it against the current catalog, embeds the Operations and stores the revision. The page reopens on the published Procedure; Plans can be engaged from it.</Step>
</Steps>
